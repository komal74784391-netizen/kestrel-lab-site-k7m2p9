---
title: "Graph Neural Imputation for Spatially Resolved Transcriptomics"
authors: "A. Roberts, N. Petrov, T. Beck"
venue: "International Symposium on Computational Genomics (ISCG 2024)"
year: 2024
status: accepted
---

Spatially resolved transcriptomics assays frequently leave a large fraction of spots with missing counts.  We formulate imputation as message passing on a joint spatial-expression graph and train a gated graph network that reconstructs dropout-prone genes while preserving spatial autocorrelation.  We benchmark the method on Slide-seq and Visium datasets and report improvements in marker-gene recovery.
