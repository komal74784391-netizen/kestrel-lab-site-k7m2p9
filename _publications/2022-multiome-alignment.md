---
title: "Joint Multiome Alignment with Anchored Factor Graphs"
authors: "A. Roberts, I. Duarte, R. Adeyemi"
venue: "Genome Informatics Conference (GIC 2022)"
year: 2022
status: published
---

Joint single-cell multiome assays produce paired measurements of chromatin accessibility and gene expression that are not directly comparable across modalities.  We propose an anchored factor graph model that couples modality-specific latent factors through a small set of shared anchor features, and derive a block-coordinate optimisation procedure for alignment.  The method recovers shared cell states across four paired multiome datasets.
