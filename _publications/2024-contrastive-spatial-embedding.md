---
title: "Contrastive Spatial Embedding Learning for Tissue Atlases"
authors: "A. Roberts, R. Adeyemi, P. Nair"
venue: "AAAI Workshop on Computational Biology (WCB 2024)"
year: 2024
status: accepted
---

We introduce a contrastive objective for learning spot-level embeddings from spatial transcriptomics atlases.  Positive pairs are sampled from local neighbourhoods and negatives from distant tissue regions, yielding embeddings that respect both expression similarity and spatial contiguity.  Downstream clustering and trajectory inference improve over autoencoder baselines on four tissue atlases.
