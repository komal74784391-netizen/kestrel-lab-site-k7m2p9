---
title: "Batched Kernel Smoothing for Temporal Point Processes"
authors: "A. Roberts, P. Nair, J. Falk"
venue: "Journal of Computational Statistics, 40(2)"
year: 2024
status: published
---

We develop a batched estimator for non-parametric kernel smoothing of intensity functions in temporal point processes.  The estimator uses block-wise sufficient statistics to obtain linear-time updates for streaming event data, and we establish consistency under mild bandwidth decay conditions.  Simulation studies and an application to server-request arrivals illustrate the computational gains.
