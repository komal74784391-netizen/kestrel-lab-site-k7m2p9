We study spectral partitioning of cell-cell affinity graphs built from single-cell expression profiles.  The paper introduces a sparsification scheme that preserves the leading eigenspace while reducing graph construction cost by an order of magnitude, together with an eigengap criterion for selecting the number of clusters without supervision. Experiments on nine scRNA-seq atlases show consistent improvements in adjusted Rand index over standard Louvain and Leiden baselines.
