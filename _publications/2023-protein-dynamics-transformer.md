---
title: "Transformer Models for Protein Conformational Dynamics"
authors: "A. Roberts, M. Tanaka, O. Chen"
venue: "Conference on Machine Learning in Structural Biology (MLSB 2023)"
year: 2023
status: accepted
---

Molecular dynamics trajectories of folded proteins exhibit long-range temporal correlations that are difficult to capture with recurrent models.  We adapt sparse transformer attention to backbone dihedral sequences and show that the learned representations reproduce experimental order parameters on a benchmark of twelve fast-folding proteins, while remaining stable at microsecond time scales.
